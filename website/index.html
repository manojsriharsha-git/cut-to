Cut To:

A free, open-source screenwriting app for Windows that works the way Final Draft does, and opens and saves your .fdx files.

Download the installer Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit Download portable zip Unzip and run, nothing to install

Free forever. No account, no subscription.

FADE IN:

1INT. YOUR DESK - NIGHT

A blank page. You type int. and it becomes a scene heading. You press Enter and you're writing action. A name in capitals becomes a character; the next line is dialogue. Real pages, Courier Prime 12 pt, one page a minute.

CUT TO:

2INT. WRITERS' ROOM - DAY

Index cards spread across the wall. Drag one and the scene moves in the script. Next door, a beat board with arrows between ideas, storylines in colour, and a profile for every character, down to what they want and what they need.

CUT TO:

3INT. PRODUCTION OFFICE - MORNING

The draft is locked. New scenes become 12A and 12B. Revisions come in blue, then pink, each changed line starred in the margin. Select a word, tag it as a prop or a vehicle, and the breakdown sheet writes itself. Shot lists and a stripboard sit next to it.

CUT TO:

4INT. TABLE READ - LATER

The script reads itself aloud, a different voice for every character. On the wall: who talks most, who talks to whom, how long every scene runs.

DISSOLVE TO:

5INT. ANY COMPUTER - ANY TIME

It opens what you already have: Final Draft, Fountain, Word, PDF, Celtx, Highland, Fade In and Trelby. It saves Final Draft and Fountain, and exports PDF, Word and RTF. Your work is copied every minute, and every save is kept, so you can compare drafts and bring old ones back.

6INT. YOUR DESK - NIGHT

Getting started takes a minute.

  1. Download the installer or the portable zip.
  2. Installer: double-click it. Portable: right-click → Extract All, open the folder, double-click Cut To.exe.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. The app isn't code-signed yet.
  4. Press Ctrl+1 and start your first scene.

Every feature and shortcut is in the user guide.
